About me

I am Swagufta Nasrin, a sound designer, filmmaker and editor from Jamia Millia Islamia, driven by the belief that stories are felt before they are understood. Studying Mass Communication gave me a complete understanding of cinema, and I found my voice in sound: in silence, texture, rhythm, and the emotions hidden between frames.

I love turning ideas into experiences. Whether through sound design, editing or filmmaking, I’m constantly exploring ways to make stories more immersive, intimate and unforgettable.

I also work as an AV designer at Design Dimensions, making audio and video for food and skincare brands.
